# Predatory Recoupment

**Principle:** An advance is a loan you repay at 10–20 cents on the dollar.
**Failure mode:** 360 deals take shares of touring/merch revenue the label didn't build; advances recoup only from the artist's fractional royalty; **cross-collateralization** ties Album A's debt to Album B's profits — permanent debt trap, Morale destroyed.
**Real case:** standard indie-label economics per the research (v2's Creative Crucible route, with debt-flow diagram).
**Mitigation:** capped recoupables, no cross-collateralization, transparent 50/50-style splits.
**In the game:** label route only — Alex brings the term sheet *believing it's good*; negotiating it is an Alex trust moment as much as a finance one.
