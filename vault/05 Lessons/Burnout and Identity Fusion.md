# Burnout and Identity Fusion

**Principle:** You are not the venture. If it must die, it should not take you with it.
**Failure mode:** total identity–venture fusion: no delegation (one person is product, support, logistics, marketing), stagnation read as personal failure, sunk-cost doubling-down instead of rational pivots; "quiet escalation" of working hours instead of structural capacity.
**Real case:** solopreneur research synthesis; the research explicitly recommends burnout imposing a *decision error rate* on subsequent turns.
**Mitigation:** treat projects as a portfolio with explicit termination thresholds; delegate before it's comfortable; rest as maintenance, not reward.
**In the game:** the Burnout stat is the player's own meter. ≥ 60: choice-consequence hints start subtly lying. ≥ 80: forced rest scene, else the [[Burnout Crash]] ending. The game *mechanically* stops being trustworthy when you are exhausted — that's the lesson.
