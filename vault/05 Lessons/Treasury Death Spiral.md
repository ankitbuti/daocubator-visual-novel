# Treasury Death Spiral

**Principle:** A treasury that's 90% your own token is a bet that you'll never have a bad month.
**Failure mode:** exploit or bear market crashes the native token exactly when cash is needed; selling to raise cash crashes it further (and trust with it) — the spiral.
**Real case:** the research's core treasury model; token price coupled to Vibes × Security, so reputation and security failures drain purchasing power too.
**Mitigation:** barbell strategy — 30–50% in uncorrelated stables/yield; pay the boring swap fees.
**In the game:** Ch2.2 sets `treasury_mix`; mono makes Ch4c eligible and adds the end-of-chapter cascade rule. Maya warns you at rel ≥ 50.
