# Governance Capture

**Principle:** If votes can be bought, someone is pricing your treasury.
**Failure mode:** 1 token = 1 vote means voting power can be bought, leased, or borrowed; wealth concentration turns "decentralized" into plutocracy; a patient whale accumulates quietly, then queues a professional-looking drain proposal.
**Real case:** **Build Finance DAO (2022)** — attacker accumulated BUILD, passed a malicious mint on a low-turnout retry, minted 1B+ tokens, drained ~160 ETH (~$470k), laundered via tumblers.
**Mitigation:** quorums, transfer time-locks, accumulation monitoring, non-token reputation weight.
**In the game:** Atlas's whole arc. The Ch4b proposal is *well-written and half the community likes it* — capture doesn't look like a heist, it looks like help.
