# The Overfunding Paradox

**Principle:** Overfunding isn't free money — it's unplanned scale on a fixed-price promise.
**Failure mode:** production complexity scales non-linearly (tooling, international logistics, certifications); if the reward price is below true at-scale unit cost, **every new backer deepens the loss**; desperation moves (retail before backers) burn the brand.
**Real case:** Coolest Cooler (2014): $50k goal → $13.2M, 62k backers, $185 reward vs. real cost near $499 retail, 20,000+ backers never fulfilled, bankruptcy + regulatory investigation.
**Mitigation:** price from real BOM + shipping + buffer; cap tiers; treat a viral spike as a logistics emergency.
**In the game:** collective route — the viral drop overfunds 300%; the game shows the unit-cost math on screen and lets you feel how *good* the big number looks.
