# The Partnership Trap

**Principle:** If your DAO has no legal wrapper, *you* are the legal wrapper.
**Failure mode:** unincorporated DAOs are general partnerships — any voting token holder can be held jointly and severally liable for the whole of damages.
**Real cases:** *Sarcuni v. bZx DAO* (partnership classification); *CFTC v. Ooki DAO* — members served via the website's chat box, default judgment, $634,542 penalty, permanent injunction.
**Mitigation:** legal wrapper before treasury (research scenario: ~$15k Swiss Verein).
**In the game:** Vera's Ch1.6 offer; declining feels principled ("we're decentralized") and returns in Ch5.2 as members being served through the Discord webhook.
