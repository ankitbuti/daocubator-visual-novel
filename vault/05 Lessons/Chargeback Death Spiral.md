# Chargeback Death Spiral

**Principle:** Your payment processor is a partner with a 1% tolerance for your mistakes.
**Failure mode:** unvetted suppliers → quality/delay complaints → disputes escalate to chargebacks → Stripe/PayPal freeze or terminate the account past ~1% chargeback rate — locking the *entire* treasury, not just the disputed orders.
**Real case:** dropshipping/e-commerce pattern per research v2 (with cascade diagram); adjacent sourcing traps: overstocking unvalidated inventory, 6-month launches becoming 18-month cash disasters.
**Mitigation:** automated dispute alerts, proactive refunds before disputes convert, vet suppliers with samples.
**In the game:** streetwear route — the 8-piece collection choice seeds it; the refund-proactively choice feels like losing money and is the survival play.
