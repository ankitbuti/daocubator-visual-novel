# Voter Apathy and Delegation

**Principle:** A governance system nobody uses is an attack surface, not a democracy.
**Failure mode:** average DAO turnout ≈ **6.3%** — malicious proposals pass on low turnout. The fix (liquid-democracy delegation) quietly re-centralizes: the top decile of voters ends up controlling up to **76.2%** of voting power — a corporate board without fiduciary duty.
**Real case:** research aggregate stats; Build Finance's capture succeeded on a low-turnout retry.
**Mitigation:** quorums, participation incentives, capped/rotating delegation, proposal legibility (plain-language summaries).
**In the game:** the apathy counter (Ch3) feeds [[Death by Democracy]]; the delegation debate makes the 76.2% pie chart a live stream moment.
