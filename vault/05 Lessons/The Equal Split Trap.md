# The Equal Split Trap

**Principle:** Equal splits are chosen to avoid a conversation, not to be fair.
**Failure mode:** 50/50 (or N-way equal) equity with no tie-breaker → operational paralysis; contributions diverge → resentment compounds.
**Real case:** Wasserman (*The Founder's Dilemmas*): 73% of startups split equity within the first month; teams that default to equal splits are ~3× more likely to be unhappy with them later.
**Mitigation:** negotiate from contribution and commitment; revisit on a schedule; pair with vesting.
**In the game:** Ch1.3 — equal split gives Morale now and detonates in Maya's Ch3 crisis; the vesting option is awkward now and load-bearing later.
