# Greenwashing Trap

**Principle:** Verify the physical asset before you financialize it.
**Failure mode:** impact DAOs "sweep the floor" buying the cheapest tokenized credits; the underlying assets are junk (decades-old projects that changed nothing); scientists and watchdogs expose it; Vibes and token price collapse together.
**Real case:** **KlimaDAO / Toucan Protocol** — Base Carbon Tonne pools revived worthless legacy offsets (2011-era Chinese hydro dams); CarbonPlan, Verra, and researchers disavowed the pools; price and trust collapsed.
**Mitigation:** independent diligence on real-world asset integrity before liquidity, even when the partnership money is good.
**In the game:** Ch5.3 — Jordan champions it for the right reasons, which is exactly why it's dangerous. Letting Maya & Spectre diligence it catches the 2011 dams.
