# Vesting and Dead Equity

**Principle:** Always vesting. Always a cliff. Always a lawyer on the founder agreement.
**Failure mode:** a co-founder leaves month 9 with their full stake ("dead equity") → survivors' incentives destroyed, VCs walk, or you spend a huge chunk of treasury buying them out.
**Real case:** standard pattern per Wasserman; the research's scenario matrix models the month-9 departure with/without vesting, and the Series A auto-rejection.
**Companion trap — 83(b):** file the IRS Section 83(b) election within 30 days of a restricted grant or get taxed on paper gains at every vest. No extensions.
**Mitigation:** 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, day-one paperwork.
**In the game:** the `equity` flag from Ch1.3 decides whether Maya's Ch3.4 departure is a sad goodbye or a legal nightmare (Treasury −20, Burnout +20). 83(b) appears as a one-line Codex gag with real teeth.
