# Research Digest

Condensed from the two PDFs in `research/` — *"The Architecture of Entrepreneurial Collapse: A Systems-Level Risk Analysis and Narrative Design Framework for Simulating Hustle Culture"* (v1 = 26pp, v2 = the "(1)" file, 19pp revision). **Neither is a superset — the game uses the union.**

## Thesis

~65% of high-potential venture failures are **internal** — people and structure, not market (Wasserman-derived). Therefore failure is teachable, and a branching visual novel is the right simulator. *(The research literally recommends the genre.)*

## The five-variable model (adopted wholesale → [[Game Systems]])

State vector: **Vibes, Treasury, Security, Morale, Burnout**, evolving via interaction matrix; token price ∝ Vibes × Security, so failures cascade (exploit → price crash → treasury collapse → missed payroll → morale crash). Burnout imposes a decision error rate.

## Failure modes → our lesson notes

DAO: governance capture, flash-loan attacks, voter apathy (6.3% turnout), delegation monopolies (top decile = 76.2% of power), partnership liability, mono-token treasury, greenwashing (v2).
Startup/creative: equal-split trap (73% split in month 1), dead equity / no vesting, 83(b) 30-day bomb, friendship hires (v1), predatory recoupment & cross-collateralization (v2).
Solopreneur: overfunding paradox (v1), sourcing/tooling squeeze (v2), chargeback death spiral / Stripe 1% line (v2), burnout & identity fusion.
OpSec: spoofed-wallet signing, ice phishing / front-end compromise, credential leakage, hot-wallet over-reliance.
→ Full pedagogical detail: [[Lessons Index]].

## Case studies (all real, all cited in Lesson popups)

Build Finance DAO ($470k capture) · Beanstalk ($181M flash loan) · Ooki/bZx (partnership liability, $634,542) · Nexus Mutual ($8M spoofed tx) · Badger DAO ($121M ice phishing) · 8ight Finance ($1.75M keys in Google Doc) · Coolest Cooler ($13.2M overfunding bankruptcy, v1) · KlimaDAO/Toucan (greenwashing collapse, v2) · Nouns veto foundation, DAOstack holographic consensus (defensive structures, v1).

## Design recommendations we adopted

Branch A (tempting shortcut → delayed catastrophe) vs Branch B (costly discipline → resilience) choice format · route structure by venture type · the hex-verification set piece · burnout error rate · vesting dead-end (40% treasury buyout or Series A rejection) · time-locks/quorums/legal-wrappers/asset-integrity/opsec as the five closing principles.
